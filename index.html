ABOUT FINDORA AI

Small things.
Big connections.

Findora AI is a school Lost and Found platform designed to make reporting and recovering belongings easier. It uses keyword-based matching to identify possible connections between lost and found reports.

The aim is simple: reduce confusion, save time and help school belongings find their way back to their owners.
